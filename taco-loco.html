Our Story

Taco Loco started as a cart with two griddles, parked outside a Denver ballpark on game days. We wanted tacos that tasted like the ones from the street stands in Mexico City: tortillas pressed by hand, and meat that had been cooking since morning.

We still do it that way. Someone is mixing masa before we open, and the al pastor turns on the spit all day. The salsas are made from fresh chiles every morning, so Tuesday's verde might be a little hotter than Monday's.
