Ken's Greatest Peaks in Colorado

I've lived in Colorado my whole life, and every time I go west on Arapahoe Road the beautiful Rocky Mountains are always in view.

My hiking experience so far is nothing special: a couple of easy trails in Boulder, and one hike that ended in a turnaround at treeline when the weather turned. My girlfriend, Jennie, says I spend way too much time picking out gear and not enough time training, and if I'm being honest, she's probably right.

Already Packed

  • Comfortable hiking boots
  • A paper map in case my phone dies
  • Fruits, nuts, and water
  • A headlamp and extra set of batteries
  • First aid kit

Right before we turned around at treeline, Jennie looked at the dark clouds and said: We're not doing this today.

Reasons I keep putting this off (ranked)

  1. Unpredictable Colorado weather
  2. Waking up early
  3. Needing too many breaks
  4. Don't have the time
  5. Procrastination
